<!-- excerpt end -->

PE Limited (Petex) has granted 10 academic licenses of the MOVE Suite Software to the Institute
of Earth Sciences, University of Lausanne, which equates to an equivalent donation of
£ 1'928'384.01.

The MOVE Suite enables three-dimensional analysis and structural modelling of deformation of
the Earth's crust through time. The licences are for teaching, tutorials and non-commercial
research in relevant Master's and PhD courses at ISTE.

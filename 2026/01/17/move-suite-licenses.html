MOVE Suite educational licences donated by PE Limited

January 17, 2026 September 04, 2026
software teaching

PE Limited (Petex) has donated ten academic licences of the MOVE Suite to the Institute of Earth Sciences, an in-kind contribution equivalent to £ 1’928’384.01.

PE Limited (Petex) has granted 10 academic licenses of the MOVE Suite Software to the Institute of Earth Sciences, University of Lausanne, which equates to an equivalent donation of £ 1’928’384.01.

The MOVE Suite enables three-dimensional analysis and structural modelling of deformation of the Earth’s crust through time. The licences are for teaching, tutorials and non-commercial research in relevant Master’s and PhD courses at ISTE.
